# Spot-gold session and technical-rule evidence: cited snippets

## Batten et al. (2018), “Does intraday technical trading have predictive power in precious metal markets?”

Source: [IDEAS/RePEc abstract and publication record](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/intfin/v52y2018icp102-113.html) (Journal of International Financial Markets, Institutions and Money, 52(C), 102–113; DOI [10.1016/j.intfin.2017.06.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intfin.2017.06.005)). Retrieved 2026-10-01. Saved source evidence: `research/sources/gap-session-01-batten-2018.md`.

> “This paper studies whether intraday technical trading rules have any significant predictive power in the precious metals market through three popular moving average rules. We find that using the standard parameters previously used in the literature, technical trading rules offer no predictive power whatsoever. However after utilising a universe of parameters, we find a number of parameter combinations offer significant predictability in the gold market, but there remains no significant predictability in the silver market.”

> “Therefore intraday technical trading rules have some predictive power in the gold market but offer no significant predictability in the silver market.”

## Jin (2022), “Performance of intraday technical trading in China’s gold market”

Source: [IDEAS/RePEc abstract and publication record](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/intfin/v76y2022ics1042443121001876.html) (Journal of International Financial Markets, Institutions and Money, 76(C); DOI [10.1016/j.intfin.2021.101481](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intfin.2021.101481)). Retrieved 2026-10-01. Saved source evidence: `research/sources/gap-session-02-china-futures-2022.md`.

> “We conduct this study by employing a 5-min intraday data from the most active futures contract listed on the Shanghai Futures Exchange covering the period spanning from 12th March 2018 to 10th March 2021.”

> “Our empirical evidence suggests that after comprehensively considering data snooping, transaction costs, market conditions, and frequencies of portfolio rebalancing, investors are unlikely to choose technical trading rules that persistently yield attractive out-of-sample performance, indicating that the predictive power of intraday technical trading in China’s gold market is illusory.”
