# Securities transaction tax amendment effective 2026-04-01

**Retrieved:** 2026-10-01  
**Official source:** [Government of India, Finance Act 2026 (Finance Bill PDF)](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/Finance_Bill.pdf)  
**Location:** Clause 143, printed page 220; amendments take effect 2026-04-01.

The official document says the previous rates were:

* sale of an option in securities: 0.1% of option premium;
* exercise of an option: 0.125% of intrinsic price;
* sale of securities futures: 0.02% of traded price.

Clause 143 amends those rates to:

* sale of an option in securities: **0.15% of option premium**;
* exercise of an option: **0.15% of intrinsic price**;
* sale of securities futures: 0.05% of traded price.

The document explicitly says the amendments take effect from 1 April 2026 and apply to tax year 2026–2027 and subsequent years. The calculator therefore uses 0.15% on the index-option sell-side premium and does not use the outdated 0.10% sale rate. Exercise/intrinsic-value STT is called out separately and is not inferred by the ordinary premium-trade preview.

The same current rate appears in Zerodha's retrieved [charges page](https://zerodha.com/charges/) under STT/CTT for options: 0.15% on sell side (on premium), and 0.15% of intrinsic value on bought options that are exercised.
